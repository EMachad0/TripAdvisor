## What other travelers usually do (research summary)
Sources: thaiholidayguide.com weekend-trips page (May 2026), madmonkeyhostels.com/post/weekend-trips-from-bangkok, continenthop.com 7-days-in-thailand, thebrokebackpacker.com Bangkok/Thailand guides, bucketlistly.blog 2-week route, culturetrip one-week-in-Bangkok.
- Consensus for one week: Bangkok 3–4 days plus ONE other region (north = Chiang Mai, or a beach), not the whole country.
- Bangkok classics nearly every itinerary has: Grand Palace + Wat Pho + Wat Arun (river side), Chinatown/Yaowarat street food, Chatuchak Weekend Market (Sat–Sun only), Khao San Road night, a rooftop bar, Muay Thai at Rajadamnern or Lumpinee, a Chao Phraya boat ride, Jodd Fairs / night markets. Hostel-crowd extras: Soi Cowboy/Nana (watch-only), Bang Krachao bike day, Lumphini Park lizards.
- Classic day/weekend trips from Bangkok: Ayutthaya (UNESCO ruins, day trip or one night), Kanchanaburi (River Kwai, Erawan Falls; best as a night), Maeklong railway market + Amphawa floating market (Amphawa Fri–Sun evenings), Khao Yai national park (nature weekend, ~2.5 h), Koh Samet (closest real beach island, ~3–4 h), Pattaya/Koh Larn (~2 h), Hua Hin.
- The bigger "extra days" options: Chiang Mai (+Pai) in the north; Krabi/Railay or Koh Phangan/Koh Tao in the south (Full Moon Party only around the full moon — check the January 2027 date).
- January events found: Fly to the Moon festival on Koh Mak, Dec 29, 2026 – Jan 2, 2027; Bangkok Music City 2027 (free, Charoenkrung, dates not out yet); big concerts at IMPACT Arena later in January (outside the window).

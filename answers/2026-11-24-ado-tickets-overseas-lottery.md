There is no first-come sale yet, and no general sale date (as of Nov 24, 2026). Tickets go by lottery. The one open now is the first that says it takes overseas residents, so enter it.

**The shows that fit the trip** are at Asue Arena Osaka: **Sat Jan 30** (doors 15:30, show 17:00 UTC+9) and **Sun Jan 31** (doors 14:30, show 16:00 UTC+9). The Tokyo shows (Jan 10–11) are before you land in Japan.

**How to enter, before Sun Nov 29, 23:59 UTC+9:**

1. Install the **Cloud Nine** app (Ado's agency; on Google Play and the App Store) and subscribe to premium: ¥1,000 a month (≈ R$ 37), charged by the app store. Cancel after the tour.
2. Create a free **Plus member ID** at Ticketplus. Type your name exactly as in your passport: the ticket carries that name, it can't be changed, and staff may ask for ID at the door.
3. In the app, tap the tour banner and pick the form for **overseas residents**. Choose Osaka and a seat type. Applying for both days, if the form allows it, raises your odds.
4. If you win, pay, then register a **face photo** at Ticketplus at least 5 days before the show. Entry is by face scan; no photo, no entry.

**Prices:** A seat ¥10,000 (≈ R$ 369), S seat ¥15,000 (≈ R$ 554), VIP ¥20,000 (≈ R$ 738, with extras). Skip Family seats: they need a child aged 3–12 with you. Rate: ¥1 = R$ 0.0369, ECB reference rate of Nov 24, 2026.

**On the day:** the ticket shows in the Ticketplus E-tickets app from about a week before, on a phone (not a tablet), tied to the phone number you applied with. If a foreign phone can't receive it, go to the e-ticket inquiry desk at the venue with your passport.

**Not confirmed:**

- Whether the app's subscription can be paid from a Brazilian store account. The listing opens for Brazil, but the payment was not tested.
- Whether the overseas form accepts a Brazilian phone number, and how it takes payment. The form is only visible inside the app.
- When results come out.
- Whether later sales will follow. On Ado's 2025 dome tour the order was fan site, Cloud Nine app, Lawson pre-request, ticket-agency presale, general sale. The Lawson rounds need a Japanese mobile number, so this app lottery may be the only one open to you.

Don't buy from resellers: the ticket is tied to the buyer's face and name, so a resold one won't get you in.

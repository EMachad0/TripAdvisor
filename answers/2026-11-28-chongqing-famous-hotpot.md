The site has one hotpot card: **Pei Jie Old Hotpot** at Hongyadong, with a solo order, the queue trick and what to say for mild. The others below have no card yet.

**Central, walkable from Jiefangbei**

- **Pei Jie Old Hotpot (珮姐老火锅)**: the name every guide lists. Beef-tallow red pot, fresh tripe, gongcai meatballs. ~¥100–120 (≈ R$ 83–99) a head. Two central branches: Hongyadong 11th floor (洪崖洞11楼), and Jiaochangkou at 5 Minsheng Road, 1st floor (民生路5号). Long queues at both; take a remote queue number before walking over.
- **Zhou Shixiong (周师兄大刀腰片火锅)**, Jiefangbei main branch, 9 Minsheng Road (民生路9号). Famous for big-knife sliced pork kidney. ¥110–130 (≈ R$ 91–107). chongqing.guide's fallback when Pei Jie's line is too long; the two are on the same street.
- **Liu Yishou (刘一手火锅), Hongyadong river-view branch**, 48 Cangbai Road (沧白路48号), at Hongyadong's top entrance. A big chain; you come for the river and stilt-house view. Price not found.
- **Yuwei Xiaoyu (渝味晓宇火锅)**, 86 Pipashan Main Street, Yuzhong. Featured on the TV series *A Bite of China*; tripe and goose intestines. ¥70–110 (≈ R$ 58–91), the cheapest of the famous ones.

**Worth the taxi for the setting**

- **Pipa Yuan Shiweixian (枇杷园食为鲜火锅)**, Nanshan, Nan'an District. Holds the Guinness record as the world's largest hotpot restaurant: 888 tables across a lantern-lit hillside. ~¥100–123 (≈ R$ 83–102). Go just before sunset.
- **Underground City (地下之城老火锅)**, 5 Ji'an Road, Yubei District, near Guanyinqiao. Inside a former air-raid shelter: rock walls, sky lanterns on the ceiling, 218 tables. ~¥100 (≈ R$ 83); China Highlights says it runs 24 hours.

Good to know:

- Guides warn the popular rooms are packed 18:00–20:00 UTC+8. Eat late or early.
- Hotpot is priced for a table: the soup base is charged per pot, so alone you pay all of it. Three or four people from the hostel make it cheaper and let you order more.
- All prices are the guides' per-head averages, not menu quotes. Opening hours were not published for any of them except Underground City; check Dianping on the day.
- Not confirmed: the older shelter hotpot **Dongting (洞亭火锅)** on Zhongshan 3rd Road, known from 2020–2022 press. I found no current address, price or hours for it.

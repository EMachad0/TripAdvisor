**What Wise charges (Brazil-issued card):** 1 free withdrawal per calendar month, of any amount within the card's limit. After that, R$ 20 per withdrawal. The ATM's bank can charge on top.

**Before leaving Brazil:**

1. Take the physical card. ATMs need the chip and the 4-digit PIN (shown in the app under the card).
2. In the Wise app, open the card, then Limits, and check that ATM withdrawals are on and the daily and monthly limits cover what you plan to take out.
3. Keep a balance in the Wise account. BRL is enough: Wise converts when the ATM charges.
4. Bring a second card from another bank as a spare, kept apart.

**At the ATM:**

- Choose "withdrawal" from a checking or debit account if asked.
- When the screen offers to charge you in BRL ("with conversion"), refuse and pick the local currency. The ATM's rate is usually 3–8% worse.
- The ATM's own fee is shown before you confirm. Cancel and try another bank if it is high.

**Which machines take foreign cards** (traveler knowledge, not checked for this trip):

- **China:** Bank of China and ICBC.
- **Japan:** Seven Bank (in every 7-Eleven) and Japan Post.
- **Korea:** machines marked "Global ATM", common in subway stations and convenience stores.
- **Thailand:** almost any, with a fee of ฿250 (≈ R$ 43) on Visa and ฿350 (≈ R$ 61) on Mastercard under the 2026 bank tables.
- **Hong Kong:** HSBC and most bank ATMs.

**Plan:** one large withdrawal per country. With five countries in about two months, three or so will be paid: around R$ 60 in Wise fees for the trip.

Rate used: 1 THB = R$ 0,173 (ECB reference rate via frankfurter.dev, 24 Nov 2026).

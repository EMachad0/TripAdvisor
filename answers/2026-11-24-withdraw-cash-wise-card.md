**Which card:** the Conta Global card is a Mastercard debit card that runs on a Wise account inside the Nubank app. Its terms list ATM withdrawals among its uses. The Ultravioleta is a credit card: cash from it is a cash advance with interest, so keep it for purchases and as the backup.

**What it costs:** Nubank's two pages disagree on the free allowance. The product page says up to R$ 1.000 a month in withdrawals without a fee; the blog (Aug 2025) says two free withdrawals a month with no value limit. Neither states the fee after that. Check it in the app before the first withdrawal: the Conta Global area shows fees and limits. Converting from BRL has zero spread for Ultravioleta clients, plus 3.5% IOF (Nubank blog, Aug 2025). The ATM's bank can charge on top.

**Before leaving Brazil:**

1. Check the physical Conta Global card is activated and you know its 4-digit PIN (in the Nubank app).
2. Add BRL to the Conta Global and convert. It holds dollars, euros and "more than 40 currencies"; see in the app whether yuan, yen, won, baht and Hong Kong dollars are on the list. A balance in dollars also works: the card converts by itself.
3. Look at the ATM withdrawal limit in the app.
4. Do one test withdrawal at the first airport, with a small amount.

**At the ATM:**

- When the screen offers to charge you in BRL or USD "with conversion", refuse and pick the local currency. The ATM's rate is usually 3–8% worse.
- The ATM's own fee is shown before you confirm. Cancel and try another bank if it is high.

**Which machines take foreign cards** (traveler knowledge, not checked for this trip):

- **China:** Bank of China and ICBC.
- **Japan:** Seven Bank (in every 7-Eleven) and Japan Post.
- **Korea:** machines marked "Global ATM", common in subway stations and convenience stores.
- **Thailand:** almost any. The card is a Mastercard, so the ATM fee is ฿350 (≈ R$ 61) under the 2026 bank tables.
- **Hong Kong:** HSBC and most bank ATMs.

**Plan:** one large withdrawal per country, which stays inside two a month for most of the route.

Rate used: 1 THB = R$ 0,173 (ECB reference rate via frankfurter.dev, 24 Nov 2026).

China's famous tea chains are mostly not bubble tea in the tapioca sense. For pearls, go to HeyTea in China and ChaTraMue in Bangkok.

- **HeyTea (喜茶), Shanghai and most big cities.** The chain that started China's new-style tea. The pearl drink is Roasted Brown Sugar Bobo Milk, 烤黑糖波波牛乳, about ¥19 (≈ R$ 17). The grape tea with cheese foam, 芝士多肉葡萄, is the other signature, about ¥18 (≈ R$ 16), no pearls. Easy branch: New World City at People's Square, 09:00–23:00 UTC+8. Also confirmed in Beijing and Chengdu.
- **Chagee (霸王茶姬), everywhere on the China route.** Milk tea made with brewed leaf, no pearls at all. Order 伯牙绝弦 (Boya Juexian, jasmine green milk tea), about ¥18 (≈ R$ 16). It is strong in caffeine: drink it before about 15:00 UTC+8, or right before a work night.
- **Chayan Yuese (茶颜悦色), Zhangjiajie and Chongqing only.** The Changsha cult brand: tea under whipped cream and crushed pecans, no pearls. Order 幽兰拿铁 (Orchid Latte), about ¥18 (≈ R$ 16). Eat the cream and nuts first, then stir. It has no real shops in Shanghai, Beijing or Xi'an.
- **ChaTraMue (ชาตรามือ), Bangkok and Chiang Mai.** The orange Thai milk tea brand since 1945. Thai milk tea ฿45 (≈ R$ 8), pearls +฿10 (≈ R$ 2): say `ใส่ไข่มุก`. Ask for `หวานน้อย` (less sweet) or 50%. Easy branch: Siam Paragon food hall by BTS Siam, 10:00–21:30 UTC+7.

In China you order in each brand's mini-program inside Alipay or WeChat (a linked foreign card works) and collect at the counter. For less sugar: 少糖 or 半糖. For a hot cup in winter: 热.

Also worth knowing: Mixue (蜜雪冰城) is the very cheap one on every corner in China, and for brown-sugar boba in Bangkok there are Bearhouse (Siam Square) and Xing Fu Tang. Korea, Japan and Hong Kong mostly have the Taiwanese chains (Gong Cha and others), nothing that needs a detour.

Prices are from 2025–2026 reports, not from each shop's menu. Rates used: ¥1 = R$ 0,8807 and ฿1 = R$ 0,173 (24 Nov 2026).

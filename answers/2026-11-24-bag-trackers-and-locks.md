**Trackers: Galaxy SmartTag2 ×2.** AirTags need an iPhone, so on the Galaxy Z Fold the SmartTag2 is the native choice (SmartThings Find). Hide one inside the 40 L bag and one in the daypack, not hanging outside. Before leaving, turn on offline finding in SmartThings Find and test it: leave a bag somewhere for an hour and check the location updates.

- The tracker only shows a new location when a nearby Galaxy phone passes by. Coverage is good in Korea, Thailand and Brazil, and weak in China: Samsung has a very small share of phones there, and Google's Find Hub does not help either because most Chinese Android phones have no Google services. In China, count on Bluetooth range only ("is my bag on this belt or in this locker"). This is an estimate, not a tested fact.
- Coin-cell trackers are allowed in checked bags (FAA and IATA guidance; Lufthansa reversed its 2022 ban). No China-specific rule against them was found (as of Nov 2026).
- They matter most on the flights where the bag is checked: West Air, Jeju Air, Peach and Greater Bay.

**Bag lock: black TSA combination lock** for the Decathlon Forclaz Travel 500's main zip (the bag takes a padlock but ships without one). TSA approval does not matter on this trip (no US flights); it just has to fit the zip pulls. Set the code at home and open and close it a few times before packing.

**Locker lock: fingerprint padlock, USB-C, IP66.** For hostel lockers in China and Thailand, which often expect your own lock (Japanese capsule hotels usually give a key).

- Specs: 5 mm shackle (fits standard locker latches), about 40 × 80 mm, 130 g, zinc alloy, works from −20 to 60 °C.
- Battery: 0.44 Wh lithium-ion (about 120 mAh), charged over USB-C at 5 V / 0.2 A. Locks of this size claim 6–12 months on standby or 1,000+ unlocks; no independent test backs that. Charge it before leaving and once more in Thailand.
- Most of these have no backup key: if it dies while locked, power it through the USB-C port from the power bank to open it.
- Enrol two fingers, in case one is cut or too dry to read in the cold.
- Keep it indoors in Harbin: the night temperatures there go below its −20 °C limit.
- 0.44 Wh is far under the 100 Wh limit for batteries built into a device, so it can fly in either bag. China's power-bank (3C) checks do not apply to it.
- It deters casual theft; a 5 mm shackle can still be cut.

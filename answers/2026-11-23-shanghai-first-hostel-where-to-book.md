**Is Dayin well located?** Yes. Straight-line distances from the hostel to the places on the Shanghai list:

- Lai Lai Xiao Long 0.2 km, the marriage market 0.5 km, Gong De Lin and the Shanghai Museum 0.9 km: all on foot.
- The Bund, Da Hu Chun and the Rockbund Christmas market about 1.5 km: a 20-minute walk down the Nanjing Road pedestrian street.
- Yu Garden 2.0 km and INS Land 2.1 km: a short metro ride or a cheap DiDi home at night.
- People's Square station (Lines 1, 2 and 8) is 320 m away. Line 2 runs to Pudong Airport, the Maglev at Longyang Road, Lujiazui and Hongqiao station for the Beijing train.
- Not close from here, or from any central hostel: Disneyland (20 km, Line 11) and the Qiantan and Jinqiao restaurants (about 10 km). 1000 Trees (3.1 km) and Jingmei noodles (3.3 km) are nearer to Desti in Jing'an.

**Trip.com or Hostelworld?** Prices for 6–9 Dec 2026, one bed, read 23 Nov 2026:

- **Dayin on Hostelworld:** ¥157.95 a night in the 8-bed male or female dorm, ¥474 (≈ R$ 421) for 3 nights. 18% deposit by card, the rest at the hostel. Free cancellation until 23:59 UTC+8 on 2 Dec; after that you lose the deposit and pay the first night.
- **Dayin on Trip.com:** from ¥355 (≈ R$ 316) for 3 nights with taxes, but the one bed shown without a sign-in was in a female 6-bed dorm with a "first stay" discount. The other dorm prices, the cancellation deadline and whether you prepay are behind the sign-in.
- **Desti on Hostelworld:** ¥334 (≈ R$ 297). **Desti on Trip.com:** ¥336 (≈ R$ 299). No difference, so Hostelworld is the simpler one there.

Sign in on Trip.com, search the same dates and look at the bed in your dorm. Book there if it is cheaper than Hostelworld and the rate says free cancellation. Trip.com also states that guests from all countries are accepted at both hostels, and the flights are already in that account. If the price is the same, Hostelworld has the smaller deposit and a known deadline.

**Other options checked (about 20 hostels):**

- **Hi Cozy Hostel Shanghai Bund:** the only one that competes. ¥378 (≈ R$ 336) for the 3 nights on Booking.com with tax, pay at the hostel in cash, free cancellation until 18:00 UTC+8 on 6 Dec. Rated 9.6 on Hostelworld (79 reviews) against Dayin's 8.8, with curtained pod bunks, and it is 940 m from the Bund next to East Nanjing Road station. Reviews are split on how social it is, and it has no bar. Hostelworld has no bed there for 6 Dec, so book it on Booking.com.
- **Jiting Hostel Nanjing West Road:** the cheap pick, from ¥225 (≈ R$ 200) for 3 nights on Trip.com, rated 9.3 from 2,798 reviews, 2 minutes from West Nanjing Road station. Mostly Chinese guests and no confirmed foreign reviewer.
- Rejected: Dayin Tianzifang (¥88 a night, but reviews describe older local residents), Hidden Garden Lujiazui (quiet, in Pudong), Dayin "People's Square & Old Town" (it is beside the railway station), WeFlow and Meego (low scores). No capsule hotel turned up in the central districts.

**Pick:** Dayin if meeting people to go out with matters most: it has the lobby bar and the biggest crowd. Hi Cozy if a better bed and ¥96 saved matter more.

**Arrival day:** the flight lands at Pudong at 05:10 UTC+8 and check-in opens at 14:00 UTC+8. Both hostels have 24-hour reception and free luggage storage, so drop the bag and go out; a bag drop at about 07:00 UTC+8 is expected but not stated on either page.

Rate: 1 CNY = R$ 0.8892 (ECB reference rate, 20 Nov 2026).

The phone is a **Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra**. The lanyard is the tether-patch kind: a thin plate sits between the phone and the case, and its loop comes out through the case's USB-C opening. It is not magnetic.

- **dbrand:** no shops anywhere, so none in Shanghai or São Paulo. Orders from abroad take 6–12 business days, and duties are paid on delivery, so a reorder is too slow for a trip.
- **Samsung's Fold8 Ultra cases** (Silicone, Carbon, Clear) are one snap-on shell for the camera half, with a closed back, no adhesive and an open notch at the USB-C port. That holds the patch. Prices on Samsung Brasil (Nov 2026): Silicone R$ 449, Carbon R$ 549, Clear Magnet R$ 269.
- **Buy for the Fold8 Ultra, not the plain Fold8.** They are different sizes.
- **Cord:** the i2GO Cordão Nylon Premium (R$ 49,90 on Mercado Livre) is 160 cm and hangs 75–130 cm. It has steel clasps and is rated for 5 kg. One review says its plate can crack over time, so carry the old patch as a spare.
- **If the case breaks on the trip:** the Ringke Onyx Flap has strap holes of its own, so it needs no patch. In China, order a Fold8 Ultra case on JD for hotel delivery; Samsung phones are rare there, so shops seldom stock them.
- **Fitting the case:** put the patch low on the back, run the loop out through the notch, and check that a USB-C cable still fits beside it.

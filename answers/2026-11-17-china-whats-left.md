**Done.** Online arrival card (receipt PDF with QR code, Nov 15). Yellow fever certificate, the digital CIVP valid for life (Nov 17). Flight in (TK280, lands at PVG on Dec 6, 05:10 UTC+8) and flight out (PN6595 from CKG on Jan 2, 15:25 UTC+8). The passport is valid to 2032.

**1. The 2027 question: decide by about Nov 25.** Visa-free entry for Brazil runs "until 24:00 on 31 December 2026". Entering on Dec 6 is fine, and the 30 days would run to Jan 5, but Jan 1–2 come after the policy's end date. No official text says whether a stay that started before the end date can continue. As of Nov 17 there is no general renewal; Russia was already extended to end-2027, and on Nov 10–12 Slovakia said China is ready to extend for it too. The last two renewals came on Nov 22, 2024 and Nov 3, 2025, so a renewal in the coming days fits the pattern (inference). If none is announced by about Nov 25, pick one:

- L tourist visa at the Chinese Visa Application Service Center in São Paulo. You are in São Paulo Nov 29 – Dec 4, so you could submit on Mon Nov 30 and ask for express. Timing is unconfirmed: about 2–3 working days express, 4–5 standard. You hand in the passport in person and get it back when the visa is ready. Fee unconfirmed, about R$ 300–700. Book the appointment on visaforchina.cn before Nov 29.
- Move the CKG → Chiang Mai flight to Dec 31. This costs a change fee and puts a travel day inside the Dec 28–31 work nights.
- Ask in writing: the 12367 immigration hotline (+86 12367) or the São Paulo consulate. An answer helps, but it is not a rule.

**2. Stays.** Nothing booked in China yet. Book Shanghai Dec 6–9 first, at places that say they accept foreign passports; hotels and hostels register you with the police automatically. A night at a friend's home must be registered within 24 hours: online via the Shanghai system or the Suishenban app, or at the local police station. It is free.

**3. Check the West Air e-ticket.** Its issuance was pending when booked. It is your proof of leaving China, and Turkish Airlines may ask for it at check-in.

**4. Phone setup before leaving Brazil.** Alipay and WeChat Pay with a card linked, a roaming eSIM or VPN (Google, WhatsApp and Instagram are blocked), Amap, and a translator with offline Chinese.

**5. Small things.**

- Count the blank passport pages: at least 2 is the safe number (not an official rule).
- Print and sign the CIVP; keep the PDF offline. Keep the arrival card QR and the flight bookings offline too.
- A power bank bought in Brazil has no 3C mark: it is stopped on flights inside China. If the route gets a domestic flight, buy a 3C-marked one there.
- At the border the purpose is tourism. The remote-work nights for a Brazilian employer are not addressed in any official text.

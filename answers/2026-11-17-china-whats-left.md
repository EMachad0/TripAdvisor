**Done (as of Nov 17).**

- Online arrival card: receipt PDF with a QR code (Nov 15). Show the QR at immigration; fill it in again at the airport if it is not found.
- Yellow fever certificate: the digital CIVP, valid for life, signed with the gov.br digital signature. Keep the PDF offline and one printout in the bag.
- Flight in: TK280, lands at PVG on Dec 6, 05:10 UTC+8. Flight out: PN6595 from CKG on Jan 2, 15:25 UTC+8; the e-ticket is issued.
- Passport valid to 2032, with 2 blank pages.
- No power bank on the trip, so the 3C rule for flights inside China does not apply.

**The Dec 31 end date: decided, no action in advance.** Visa-free entry for Brazil runs "until 24:00 on 31 December 2026", and Jan 1–2 come after it. No official text says whether a stay that started before that date can continue; as of Nov 17 no renewal for 2027 had been announced (Russia already has one, and Slovakia says it was offered one). The plan:

- If it is renewed: nothing changes.
- If not: the bet is that the entry date counts. The 30 days run from the day after entry, so a Dec 6 entry is legal to Tue Jan 5, and leaving Jan 2 is day 27.
- If Turkish Airlines or Shanghai immigration refuse on Dec 6 because the stay runs past Dec 31: book a flight out of China on or before Dec 31 and leave early.
- If stopped when leaving Chongqing on Jan 2: contact the Brazilian embassy or consulate in China.

**At the border, have ready:** passport, the arrival card QR, the yellow fever certificate, the PN6595 booking and the first hotel's booking. The purpose of the visit is tourism.

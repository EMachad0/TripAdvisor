China is the first stop and the place where tissues matter most: many public toilets have no paper and no soap, least of all in small restaurants, markets, bus stations and the countryside. Tourist sights and malls are better.

- **From Brazil:** two pocket packs, in the day backpack. They cover the flights and the first days.
- **There:** convenience stores and street stands sell pocket packs everywhere in China, Korea, Japan, Thailand and Hong Kong. Keep two packs in the day bag at all times.
- Some Chinese packs are menthol scented. Check the pack if that bothers you.
- Add a small hand sanitizer (under 100 ml for the cabin), since soap is often missing too.

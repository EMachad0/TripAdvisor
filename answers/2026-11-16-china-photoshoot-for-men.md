There are two kinds, and both take men.

**Costume shoots.** You rent a historical outfit, get hair and makeup done, and a photographer shoots you at a palace, a city wall or an old street. Trip.com's guide says most studios keep a few men's styles, usually Ming or Tang cuts, and the choice is smaller than for women. Ask to see the men's rack before you pay.

- Xi'an is the place for Tang looks and the cheapest. Trip.com's guide puts a Tang shoot with makeup, hair and 8 edited photos at about ¥270 (≈ R$ 228), which it gives as US$40.
- In Beijing, rental alone runs ¥150–200 (≈ R$ 127–170). A studio near Wangfujing charges about ¥500 (≈ R$ 424) for an hour with a photographer, around 60 photos and 12 edits.
- Fenghuang's costume-photo boards advertise "all-in ¥199" (≈ R$ 169) and then add extras. Agree on what's included first.

**A photographer who follows you.** No costume. You wear your own clothes and they shoot you around the sights for an hour or two. Nothing about this is aimed at women, and on a solo trip it's the easiest way to get good photos of yourself.

- Chengdu, summer 2025: about ¥150 (≈ R$ 127) an hour at the IFS panda, or about ¥200 (≈ R$ 170) with retouching. Two hours at IFS and Chunxi Road cost ¥300–400 (≈ R$ 255–340). On Kuanzhai Alley, costume, makeup and shoot together cost ¥100–150 (≈ R$ 85–127). A full day ran about ¥1,000 (≈ R$ 849).
- Chongqing's Liziba station has roadside photographers at about ¥20 (≈ R$ 17) a shot. For Hongyadong at night or the New Year's Eve crowd, a follow photographer does better.

**How to find one.** Search Xiaohongshu (RED) for the city plus 男生旅拍 (men's travel shoot) or 男士跟拍, for example 重庆 男生旅拍, and pick someone whose portfolio has men in it. Dianping lists studios with reviews. Trip.com and Klook sell English-language packages at higher prices. In a DM, send 可以拍男生吗？ (do you shoot men?) and 多少钱一小时，几张精修？ (how much an hour, how many edited photos?).

Agree on the hours, the number of raw and edited photos and the delivery date before you start. The Chengdu article reports surcharges for extra retouching, and these photographers need no licence.

Prices use 1 CNY = R$ 0.8489 (ECB rate, 2026-11-13).

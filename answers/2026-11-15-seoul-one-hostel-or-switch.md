**Why not one dorm for nine nights.** You work 21:00–06:00 UTC+9 on Mon 11, Tue 12, Wed 13, Thu 14, Fri 15, Mon 18 and Tue 19 Jan, with video meetings, and then sleep until early afternoon. Only Sat 16 and Sun 17 are free nights. None of the hostels checked says a lounge is open all night or that calls are allowed there, and every one has an 11:00 UTC+9 check-out with cleaning around it.

**The plan (three bookings, two 10-minute walks):**

1. **Mon 11 – Fri 15 (5 nights): Oppa Hostel, Sinchon, single room with its own bathroom.** ₩47,000 (≈ R$ 199) a weeknight and ₩74,260 (≈ R$ 315) on Fri 15; ₩262,260 (≈ R$ 1.111) for the five. Reviews mention fast wifi and quiet nights. It is not social.
2. **Sat 16 + Sun 17 (2 nights): Time Travelers Relax Guesthouse, Hongdae, single room.** ₩58,825 (≈ R$ 249) a night, ₩117,650 (≈ R$ 498). Move on Saturday at 11:00 UTC+9, after sleeping off the Friday shift; its check-in opens at 12:00 UTC+9, so you can sleep again before going out. Social but alcohol-free and "not a party place". The dorm bed is ₩29,412 (≈ R$ 125) if you would rather save.
3. **Mon 18 + Tue 19 (2 nights): back to Oppa.** ₩50,000 (≈ R$ 212) a night. Check-in is from 15:00 UTC+9; it keeps luggage for free.

**No-move option:** Oppa's single for all 9 nights as one booking is ₩496,320 (≈ R$ 2.102), only ₩16,410 more. Hongdae's clubs are a 20–30 minute walk or a short taxi. Pick this if moving twice sounds like a chore.

**The other hostels on the list:**

- **Lazy Cube:** you can't take meetings in the pod. The capsules are open-fronted boxes with a curtain, in a 70-bed room. It has a lounge one floor up, but no source says it is open all night. Fine for one or two nights just for the capsule: ₩45,000 Sun–Thu, ₩81,900 Fri and Sat.
- **Time Travelers Relax for the work nights:** no. Reviews report bad wifi, thin walls, no table to work at and a wake-up knock at 10:50 UTC+9.
- **Jin's Paradise:** nothing on sale after Tue 5 Jan, and it has no January or February reviews in three years, so it probably closes then. Not confirmed.
- **Original Backpackers (Dongdaemun):** the best work setup on paper, but 11–15 Jan are sold out. From 16 Jan only twins at about ₩118,000 a night.
- **G Guesthouse (Itaewon):** common rooms close by midnight, and it is across town.
- **Bunk Guesthouse Hongdae** (Booking.com, pub downstairs, 8.9 from 1,735 reviews) is the more social weekend alternative: dorm about ₩40,000 on Sat 16.

**Before you book:**

- Each single room showed **1 left** on 15 Nov. Free cancellation: Oppa until 3 Jan 2027, Time Travelers until 8 Jan 2027.
- Both list a **10% surcharge for paying by card**. Bring cash for the balance.
- Time Travelers sells Fri 15 and Sat 16 only as part of a stay of 2+ nights, so search 16 → 18 Jan, not one night.
- Message Oppa first to ask whether night video calls are fine in the room and whether you can drop bags early on Mon 11. No review covers either.
- **Arrival day:** you land at 08:40 UTC+9 and reach Sinchon around 11:00 UTC+9, four hours before check-in. Either book Sun 10 Jan too (+₩47,000, ≈ R$ 199) so the room is ready, or shower and nap at the 24-hour sauna by Hongik Univ. exit 4 (about ₩12,000, from blogs).
- **Saturday night vs Hwacheon on Sunday:** the festival bus leaves Dong Seoul at 06:45 UTC+9, and a foreigner shuttle (Trazy) picks up at Hongik Univ. exit 4 at 08:30 UTC+9. A club night until 05:00 and the festival the same morning don't fit. Do the pub crawl, be in bed by about 02:00 UTC+9 and take the 08:30 shuttle, or drop one of the two.

Prices are Hostelworld's for one guest, read on 15 Nov 2026, and they move. R$ at 1 KRW = R$ 0,004236 (ECB reference rate of 13 Nov 2026).

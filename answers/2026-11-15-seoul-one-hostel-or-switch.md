**Why one hostel, and why Hongdae.**

- Your night-time must-dos (club night, pub crawl, Hongojib BBQ, T1 Base Camp) are all within about 1 km of Hongik Univ. station, and there are no subway trains between about midnight and 05:30 UTC+9.
- The daytime sights (palaces, markets, Lotte World Tower, Bongeunsa) are 4–16 km away from every hostel checked, so no second base brings them close. They are a subway ride from anywhere.
- The airport train stops at Hongik Univ. station, for both the arrival on Mon 11 and the 22:50 UTC+9 flight on Wed 20.
- You work 21:00–06:00 UTC+9 on seven of the nine nights and sleep until early afternoon. Every move costs an 11:00 UTC+9 check-out in the middle of that sleep.
- Bunk only sells stays of 2+ nights on these dates, and people you meet in the first days are the ones you go out with on Saturday.

**Bunk Guesthouse Hongdae** (prices for one adult on Booking.com, read 15 Nov 2026):

- 6-bed mixed dorm: ₩36,000 (≈ R$ 152) Sun–Thu, ₩40,000 (≈ R$ 169) on Fri 15 and Sat 16. Nine nights: ₩332,000 (≈ R$ 1.406), or ₩298,800 signed in to Booking's Genius. Only 2 beds were left.
- Private twin with its own bathroom: ₩100,000 (≈ R$ 424) Sun–Thu, ₩134,000 (≈ R$ 568) Fri and Sat.
- Each bunk has a dark curtain, lamp and socket. Cleanliness 9.1, cleaned daily, free breakfast 08:00–10:00 UTC+9.
- 4–8 minutes on foot to Hongik Univ. station, about 10 to the club street.
- Check-in 15:00–21:00 UTC+9, check-out by 11:00 UTC+9; bags can be left before check-in. Free cancellation until 27 Dec 2026.
- Friendly rather than a party hostel: people meet at breakfast and in the lounge. No recent review shows guests going out clubbing together, so use the Saturday pub crawl for that.

**Meetings.** Quiet hours are 00:00–07:00 UTC+9 and the lounge rule is no talking after midnight. Meetings where you speak fit in the lounge if they end by 00:00 UTC+9 (12:00 in São Paulo). If they run later, book the private room for those nights in blocks of two: Mon 11 + Tue 12 and Mon 18 + Tue 19 as private with the dorm in between comes to ₩588,000 (≈ R$ 2.491). No source says whether the lounge stays open all night for silent laptop work: ask the hostel.

**Why not the others:**

- **Zzzip Guesthouse** (the most social, atmosphere 98) and **Jin's Paradise** (98): nothing on sale for these nights. Both look closed for a winter break; neither has confirmed it.
- **Time Travelers Relax:** a dorm bed for the 9 nights is ₩264,708 (≈ R$ 1.121), the cheapest social option, but reviews report bad wifi, few curtains and no table to work at.
- **Oppa Hostel (Sinchon):** quiet single rooms from ₩47,000, but not social.
- **Lazy Cube:** capsules with a curtain in a 70-bed room, ₩81,900 on Fri and Sat, "not the most social".
- **Original Backpackers (Dongdaemun):** 11–15 Jan sold out. **G Guesthouse (Itaewon):** across town, common rooms close by midnight.

**Also plan for:**

- **Arrival day:** you reach Hongdae around 11:00 UTC+9, four hours before check-in, with a shift that night. Drop the bags and nap at the 24-hour sauna by Hongik Univ. exit 4 (about ₩12,000, from blogs).
- **Day sleep in a dorm:** breakfast, packing and check-out happen around you. Bring earplugs and an eye mask.
- **Saturday night vs Hwacheon on Sunday:** the festival bus leaves Dong Seoul at 06:45 UTC+9 and a foreigner shuttle picks up at Hongik Univ. exit 4 at 08:30 UTC+9. A club night until 05:00 does not fit with either.

**Dates:** the Jeju Air flight from Singapore lands at Incheon at 08:40 UTC+9 on Mon 11 Jan 2027. Book check-in **Mon 11 Jan**, check-out **Wed 13 Jan**: the nights of the 11th and 12th. Two nights is also the minimum the guesthouse sells.

**Room:** take the private room for these two nights. Both are work nights (21:00–06:00 UTC+9), the first one comes straight after a red-eye, and Monday has a call where you speak. The house rules ban talking in the lounge after 00:00 UTC+9, and day sleep in a dorm is broken by roommates packing until the 11:00 UTC+9 check-out. If the budget matters more, the 6-bed mixed dorm is the one to pick.

Prices for the 2 nights on Booking.com, breakfast included, read 23 Nov 2026:

- **Twin Room with Bunk Bed** (private, own bathroom, for 1): ₩200,000 (≈ R$ 887), or ₩180,000 (≈ R$ 798) signed in. 2 left.
- **Bed in 6-Bed Mixed Dormitory**: ₩72,000 (≈ R$ 319), or ₩64,800 (≈ R$ 287) signed in. 6 left.
- Bed in 4-Bed Mixed Dormitory: ₩76,000 (≈ R$ 337), or ₩68,400 (≈ R$ 303) signed in. 4 left.

The private room costs ₩128,000 (≈ R$ 567) more than the dorm bed for the two nights. Rate: 1 KRW = R$ 0.004433 (ECB reference rate, 20 Nov 2026).

**Where:** [Booking.com, 11–13 Jan, 1 adult](https://www.booking.com/hotel/kr/bunk-guesthouse-hongdae.en-gb.html?checkin=2027-01-11&checkout=2027-01-13&group_adults=1&no_rooms=1&group_children=0&selected_currency=KRW).

1. Sign in first (a free account gives the 10% members price).
2. Pick the rate with free cancellation: free until 27 Dec 2026, nothing charged until 26 Dec 2026.
3. Let Booking.com charge the card. Paying at the desk with a foreign card adds a 5–10% surcharge.

[Agoda](https://www.agoda.com/bunk-guesthouse-hostel/hotel/seoul-kr.html?checkIn=2027-01-11&los=2&adults=1&rooms=1&currency=KRW) also sells it and showed "from ₩28,030" for these dates, but its room list did not load, so which bed that is, and whether tax is included, is not confirmed. Hostelworld sells no beds here.

**For the K-ETA:** the guesthouse address is the same whichever bed you take, so any of these bookings works for the form. If the stay changes later, update the K-ETA (free). The other 7 nights (13–20 Jan) can go in a second booking; each booking needs at least 2 nights.

Don't book **Bunk Backpackers Guesthouse** by mistake: it is a different listing at another address.

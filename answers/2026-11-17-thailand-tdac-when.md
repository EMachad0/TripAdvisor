The Immigration Bureau's rule is to submit within 3 days before arrival. In practice the official form's date picker offers today plus the next 3 days, by the Thai date. Tested on the real form on Nov 17 at night in Brazil (already Nov 18 in Thailand): it offered Nov 18–21 and nothing later.

- **Window for this trip:** Wed Dec 30 to Sat Jan 2, Thai dates (lands CNX 17:15 UTC+7 on West Air PN6595). In Chongqing that opens at 01:00 UTC+8 on Dec 30. Do it on Dec 30 or 31, with the VPN or roaming eSIM on.
- **Where:** only tdac.immigration.go.th or the official THIM app. It's free.
- **Early "TDAC" sites** (for example tdac.agents.co.th, about US$8 for early requests) are private agents. They hold your data and submit it inside the same 3-day window, so they gain you nothing.
- **Have ready:** passport, the flight number, the first address in Chiang Mai (book it before Dec 30), purpose Holiday, and the onward flight SQ 707 on Jan 10.
- **After:** screenshot the QR or confirmation and keep it offline.
- **Forgot?** Help points at the airport reportedly let you file on arrival (unconfirmed). Don't count on it.

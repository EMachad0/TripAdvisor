The Immigration Bureau's rule is to submit within 3 days before arrival, counting the arrival day. Users report that the official form's date picker won't take an arrival date further out.

- **Window for this trip:** Thu Dec 31 to Sat Jan 2 (lands CNX 17:15 UTC+7 on West Air PN6595). Do it in Chongqing on Dec 31 or Jan 1, with the VPN or roaming eSIM on.
- **Where:** only tdac.immigration.go.th or the official THIM app. It's free.
- **Early "TDAC" sites** (for example tdac.agents.co.th, about US$8 for early requests) are private agents. They hold your data and submit it inside the same 3-day window, so they gain you nothing.
- **Have ready:** passport, the flight number, the first address in Chiang Mai (book it before Dec 31), purpose Holiday, and the onward flight SQ 707 on Jan 10.
- **After:** screenshot the QR or confirmation and keep it offline.
- **Forgot?** Help points at the airport reportedly let you file on arrival (unconfirmed). Don't count on it.

**Chengdu: 听·江湖古风酒馆（玉林店）.** "Listen · Jianghu, ancient-style tavern". Address to show a driver: 成都市武侯区玉林中路13号1楼 (the street number comes from search snippets only). Douyin posts also call it 民乐酒馆 and 民谣酒馆.

- The theme is the *jianghu*, the wandering-swordsman world of wuxia novels. A visitor's video shows ancient-style dance; a 2026 marketing report adds folk songs, classical dance and pipa every night (not confirmed).
- Hours 19:00–02:00 UTC+8 and ¥93–97 (≈ R$ 79–82) a head, both unconfirmed. No cover charge found, no menu with prices found.
- Open in 2026 per an October post and a January video, but no map listing could be opened. Check Dianping or its Douyin account before going. Nothing is known about Dec 23–26.
- Local crowd, Mandarin songs, no English found. Good for an hour or two on a Yulin evening, weak for meeting people.
- The only other branch is in Qionglai, a satellite city of Chengdu. No branch in any other city.

**Xi'an: 我命由我·唐宫酒馆中国菜.** 西安市雁塔区雁翔路3369号曲江创意谷 L层, Metro Line 5 Huangqutou, Exit B, then about 730 m.

- A Tang-palace restaurant, not a bar: hanfu dancers on a stage at mealtimes, about every half hour at peak (reviews to Oct 2026). Whether any music is live is not confirmed.
- About ¥66 (≈ R$ 56) a head, daily 10:00–22:00 UTC+8, no show fee mentioned. Ask for the main hall; the stage is not visible from the private rooms.
- Not worth an evening of a two-night stop on its own. Dinner, one show, then a bar.

**No verified match in the other cities.** Dianping, Xiaohongshu and Amap could not be read, so a small place may exist that this search missed. Closest finds:

- Chongqing: 道藏·天枢·国风酒馆, 大坪时代天街A馆2号楼1002室, 19:30–00:30 UTC+8, open in 2026. A quiet guofeng tea-and-liquor bar, no music or hanfu.
- Beijing: 江湖酒吧, 东棉花胡同7号. Open in 2026, a hutong courtyard live-music bar. Jazz and folk, the jianghu is only in the name.
- Shanghai: nothing open that fits. 1945中国酒馆 on the Rockbund looks closed.

Two Samsung Wallet launches in late 2026 are probably behind the rumour. Neither covers a Samsung Wallet set up in Brazil.

- **Visa Scan to Pay (launched 16 Nov 2026):** Samsung Wallet scans merchant QR codes in mainland China, Hong Kong, Malaysia and Vietnam. Only for Samsung Wallet users in Singapore and Australia with a Visa card. In mainland China it runs on the Lakala network, not Alipay or WeChat Pay. Visa says more countries will follow "over the next few quarters", with no names or dates.
- **Samsung Wallet Money (announced 27 Oct 2026):** pays at WeChat Pay merchants in China by QR code, only for Korean Galaxy users (a Samsung, Tencent and Woori Bank deal). Linking credit cards and tap-to-pay are planned for Q1 2027, also only for Korea.
- **Google Wallet:** no announcement of China support was found, and mainland China is not a Google Wallet market. Guides say tapping a foreign card mostly fails at ordinary shop terminals, because China pays by QR code, not NFC.

**Where a tap may work: metro gates.** Guides say Beijing (since June 2025) and Shanghai (since 28 June 2025) take foreign Visa and Mastercard contactless cards at the gates. Whether the same card inside Google Wallet or Samsung Wallet works there is disputed between sources. Try it if you like, but count on the physical card or the Alipay transit code.

**What to do.** Link the card in Alipay and in WeChat Pay before leaving Brazil. If Visa Scan to Pay reaches Brazil later, it would show up in Samsung Wallet as a QR option on a Visa card.

**Can Alipay or WeChat Pay be added to Samsung Wallet?** No. Samsung Wallet in Brazil takes Pix and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Elo, Amex, Diners, Hipercard); there is no option to add an Alipay or WeChat account. The one time Samsung did this was Samsung Pay in mainland China in 2016, for phones sold there. It also doesn't work the other way: Alipay and WeChat Pay want the card itself typed in, not a card held in Samsung Wallet.

**Making Alipay as quick as the wallet.** On the Galaxy, set Settings › Advanced features › Side button › Double press › Open app › Alipay. Long-pressing the Alipay or WeChat icon should also offer Scan and Pay shortcuts that can be dragged to the home screen (not tested on the Fold8 Ultra).

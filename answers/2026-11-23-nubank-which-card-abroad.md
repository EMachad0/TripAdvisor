The two cards cost about the same before rewards, going by Nubank's own pages:

- **Ultravioleta credit:** 3.5% spread. The 3.5% IOF is charged and then refunded on the bill within 7 days. Purchases earn 1.25% cashback or 2.2 points per dollar. Net cost about 2.25%.
- **Conta Global debit (Mastercard, runs on Wise):** zero spread, 3.5% IOF on each conversion, not refunded, no cashback. Net cost about 3.5%.

**Why credit is the main card.** It comes out about 1.25% cheaper, needs no pre-loading and leaves no leftover balance in a currency you have left behind. Each purchase takes the exchange rate of the day you spend.

**What the Conta Global card is for.**

- Cash: two free ATM withdrawals a month abroad. Never withdraw on the credit card; that is a cash advance.
- Backup: link it as the second card in Alipay and WeChat Pay, in case the credit card is declined or blocked.
- Locking a rate: convert ahead only if you expect the real to weaken.

**In China.** Alipay and WeChat Pay add their own 3% on payments over ¥200 with any foreign card. Split bigger bills when the shop allows it.

**Not confirmed.**

- Nubank's pages do not say which reference rate the 3.5% credit spread sits on. Other sites say PTAX; the Conta Global uses the commercial rate. The 1.25% gap is arithmetic on Nubank's figures, not a Nubank statement.
- Nubank says the Conta Global holds "more than 40 currencies" without listing them. Check in the app for yuan, yen, won, baht and Hong Kong and Singapore dollars. Paying in a currency you do not hold converts from another balance at a rate Nubank does not publish.
- No first-hand report was found of a Nubank card inside Alipay or WeChat Pay. Both take Mastercard credit and debit.
- These figures come from Nubank blog posts, not the fee schedule. The rate shown in the app at the moment of purchase or conversion is the real one.

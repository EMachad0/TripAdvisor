**Where it stands (as of Nov 24, 2026).**

- The newest visa notice on the Chinese embassy in Brazil's site is still the one from Nov 4, 2025: visa-free for Brazil and 44 other countries until 24:00 UTC+8 on Dec 31, 2026, up to 30 days per stay.
- Two trackers that follow the official notices, both last checked Nov 19, say no extension has been published.
- On Nov 17 the Foreign Ministry spokesperson said China is "seriously studying the relevant policy" (reported by a travel site; the transcript was not read).
- Only Russia has a 2027 date so far. Slovakia's foreign minister said on Nov 10 that China is ready to extend for Slovaks, but China has published nothing.

**When to expect it.** The last two renewals came on Nov 22, 2024 and Nov 3, 2025. This year is already later than both, so a notice could come any day, or not at all.

**What it means for this trip.** Entry is Dec 6 and exit is Jan 2. The entry is inside the policy either way. The open point is the two days after Dec 31:

- No official notice says whether a stay that started before Dec 31 can run past it. The two trackers disagree: one says the 30 days run from the entry date, the other says not to assume so.
- The plan from Nov 17 stands: do nothing in advance, and leave China by Dec 31 only if the airline or immigration refuses on Dec 6.
- For a definite answer, ask the embassy's consular section in Brasília by email (the address is on the embassy's visa page).

**Check again** around Dec 1, a few days before flying.

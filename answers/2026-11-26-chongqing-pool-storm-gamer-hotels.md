All prices were read on 26 Nov 2026 for one adult, taxes included, and will move. BRL uses 1 CNY = R$ 0,8656 and 1 USD = R$ 5,765 (ECB reference rates, 26 Nov 2026).

**The storm shower: SEYA Panoramic Hotel, Jiefangbei Flagship (世野SEYA酒店 解放碑店)**

- 58F Yingli IFC, 28 Minquan Road, Yuzhong, 130 m from Jiaochangkou metro (Lines 1 and 2).
- The "Storm Spa" (暴风雨SPA) is a shared hydrotherapy room in the hotel, not a shower inside the bedroom: heavy overhead rain, water jets, thunder and lightning effects. Every room rate on Trip.com includes one session per stay.
- Every room has a pool-style soaking tub by the window with the skyline behind it.
- Trip.com, night of 28 Dec 2026: Twilight Tower River View King ¥1,738 (≈ R$ 1.504), Dawn Tower King with 3 m hot tub ¥1,798 (≈ R$ 1.556). Free cancellation until 18:00 UTC+8 on the day.
- Booking.com, night of 29 Dec: ¥2,086 (≈ R$ 1.806). New Year's Eve: ¥2,633 (≈ R$ 2.279). Booking.com lists cash as the only payment method, so Trip.com is the better seller.
- Foreign guests are accepted, with many foreign reviews.
- Not confirmed: session hours (social posts say 10:00–20:00 UTC+8, booked ahead) and whether a solo guest gets the room alone.
- If the clip showed lightning across the bedroom walls rather than falling water, it was the sister hotel ISEYA Panoramic at Raffles Plaza (30F, 1 Xinhua Road), which has projection suites. No price checked.

**Other rooms with a private pool or tub**

- **YOLO REEF Private Onsen Hotel** (YOLO礁屿私汤酒店), 229 Jiefang West Road, about 1.5 km from Jiefangbei: a private hot-spring pool by the window facing the river, floors 22–40. Opened 2026, takes foreign guests. No price found, Trip.com shows it only after login.
- **Yolo Private Hot Spring Designer Hotel, Nanbin Road** (Nan'an, by Houbao metro): river-view rooms with private pool and projector. No price found.
- **Conrad Chongqing** (Nan'an, about 15 minutes by taxi): river-view room with bathtub ¥1,585 (≈ R$ 1.372) non-refundable or ¥1,864 (≈ R$ 1.613) refundable on 29 Dec. A bathtub, not a pool.
- **Vista High-Rise River View Hotel** (45F, 328 Xinhua Road): every room has a bath; Superior Queen ¥985 (≈ R$ 853), Deluxe Suite ¥1,644 (≈ R$ 1.423) on 29 Dec.
- **Hot-spring resorts in Beibei**, about 1 hour by car: Bolian Resorts (every room has an outdoor spring) and Banyan Tree Beibei (every villa has a pool). No seller price found for either, and Banyan Tree's own pages were down in Nov 2026.

**Gamer hotels (电竞酒店)**

Prices are Trip.com's cheapest room for the night of 28 Dec 2026, quoted in USD with a first-stay discount applied.

1. **Guangzhi GLETOH E-sports Hotel, Jiefangbei** (光致·GLETOH电竞酒店), 51 Minquan Road, 150 m from Jiaochangkou metro: RTX 4070, 240 Hz monitors. Twin from US$35 (≈ R$ 202), full price US$63. ¥200 deposit.
2. **YEP E-sports Hotel, Jiefangbei** (YEP·页谱电竞旅居酒店), Ruifu Plaza, 40 m from Jiaochangkou metro: RTX 4060, 2K 165–185 Hz. A single-person room from US$14 (≈ R$ 81), but 15 m², no window and shared bathrooms. A German guest reviewed it in Oct 2026.
3. **Guangzhi Cloud View Riverfront** (光致·拾鎏云端江景电竞酒店), 186 Beibin 1st Road, Jiangbei: RTX 5070 at 240–320 Hz, and hot-spring river-view suites with RTX 5080 and a 4K monitor. Twin from US$40 (≈ R$ 231). No price found for the suites.
4. **Guangzhi Guanyinqiao** (光致电竞酒店 观音桥店): has a single room with RTX 5060; an Australian couple stayed in Nov 2026. From US$34 (≈ R$ 196).

- **Avoid WanYoo Esports Hotel Jiefangbei** (网鱼电竞酒店): best PCs in town, but its policy says mainland Chinese ID cards only.
- Not confirmed: whether the room PCs need a Chinese-ID login. Ask in the Trip.com chat before booking: `外国护照可以入住并使用电脑吗？可以登录自己的Steam账号吗？`
- Expect Chinese Windows and Chinese game launchers. The Steam client usually logs in and plays, but the store and community pages are blocked. Battle.net runs through NetEase in China and foreign accounts have failed to log in.

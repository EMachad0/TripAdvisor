**Arrival card.** The official notice only says it can be filled in "before coming to China" and gives no window. Travel guides say the form takes arrival dates up to 3 days ahead; this is not confirmed officially. For a landing on Sun Dec 6, fill it in from Thu Dec 3. It is not needed to board: it can also be done at the airport by QR code, on a kiosk or on paper. Use only the official form (the NIA warns about fake paid sites) and save a screenshot of the result.

**Tickets.** No official rule asks for paper. The visa-free FAQ only recommends carrying flight and hotel bookings that match the trip. What matters is proof of the flight out of China within 30 days.

- The booking confirmation or e-ticket receipt exists from the day of purchase, in the email and in the Trip.com app. The boarding pass is the part that only appears at check-in, and it is not needed as proof.
- Save the confirmation as a PDF or screenshot for offline use; foreign apps and email can be slow or blocked in China.
- One paper copy is a free backup if the phone dies. It is optional.

**Power bank.** Since 2025-06-28, flights inside mainland China do not accept power banks without a clear 3C mark, or from recalled batches. A power bank bought in Brazil will not have the mark.

- Flights into and out of China: reports say the 3C mark is not checked on international flights. This comes from Chinese news reports, not from the aviation authority's own text, and some travelers report checks at Beijing and Shanghai.
- Trains: allowed up to 100 Wh, with no 3C check.
- Flights inside China: it is stopped at security. Airports offer storage or mailing, which does not help on a one-way route.
- Always: carry-on only, 100 Wh at most (about 27,000 mAh), with the capacity printed on it.
- If the route gets a domestic flight, buy a 3C-marked power bank in China and leave the Brazilian one out of that flight.

**Yellow fever certificate: required.** China asks for it from travelers aged 9 months or over arriving from a country with yellow fever risk, and Brazil is one (WHO list, CDC). The vaccine must be taken at least 10 days before arrival, and the international certificate (CIVP) is issued free by Anvisa on gov.br. It is checked only sometimes, but carry it.

**Also settled.**

- The health declaration form was scrapped on 2023-11-01. Only travelers with symptoms declare.
- The 30 days count from the day after entry: for a Dec 6 entry the last legal day is Tue Jan 5, 2027.
- The passport only has to be valid for the trip; no 6-month rule is stated.

**Still open (as of 2026-11-15).** The visa-free policy is written to end on 2026-12-31 and no renewal has been announced. No official text says whether someone who entered before that date can stay past it. The last two renewals were announced on 2024-11-22 and 2025-11-03.

What the cheapest room, ¥1.250 (≈ R$ 1.061) on the night of 27 Dec 2026, includes (as of Nov 2026, rate 1 CNY = R$ 0,8489 of 13 Nov):

- **The room:** 36 to 46 m² depending on the type, soundproofed, with a city view on Booking.com and "skyline view" on the Marriott site. A river view costs ¥117 more.
- **A bathtub:** yes. Booking.com lists a bath and a separate shower in every room type.
- **The pool:** indoors and heated, with a hot tub (Booking.com). Trip.com describes it as a 54th-floor pool with views over the city. It is not an open-air infinity pool. One listing gives the hours as 06:00–23:00 UTC+8.
- **Also in the price:** the gym. A spa and sauna are listed, but no source says whether they cost extra.
- **Not in the price:** breakfast (¥59 more on the breakfast rate) and the executive lounge, which comes only with club rooms.

Is the pool cold in December? It is indoors and listed as heated, so the weather outside (6–12 °C) does not reach it. No source gives the water temperature, and hotel pools in China are often kept cooler than a hot tub, so expect a swimming temperature, not a warm bath. Ask the hotel for the temperature before booking if the pool is the main reason to go.

If an open-air infinity pool is what you want, Chongqing has some (the Conrad's is on the 55th floor, the InterContinental Raffles City has a 25 m one), but none of the sources say they are heated or open in winter.

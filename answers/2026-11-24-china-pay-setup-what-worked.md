**What is set up.** Alipay and WeChat Pay both hold the Ultravioleta credit card, and the WeChat identity check with the passport passed. All of it was done in Brazil. Amap is installed with an account; it is clunky, but sharing an address with a friend worked.

**What was tried and refused.**

- **Tipping a friend's reward code (赞赏码) in WeChat:** refused. The app says "International bank cards don't support Receive Money payments".
- **Topping up the WeChat balance:** refused, and the reason shown was the phone number, not the card. Guides say a foreign card cannot top up the balance in either app.

**The rule.** A card issued outside mainland China pays merchants: shops, restaurants, taxis, metro, mini-programs. It does not pay people. That covers a person's own receive or reward code, transfers and red packets, in WeChat and in Alipay, with a credit or a debit card.

**Paying a person anyway.**

1. Get yuan into the balance first: a friend with a Chinese account sends a transfer or a red packet. Then pay the personal code from Balance. Receiving on a passport-verified foreign account is reported to work but was not tested.
2. Splitting a bill: pay the restaurant with the card and let the friends send their share. That fills the balance for later.
3. Cash: withdraw yuan with the Conta Global card and keep ¥200–300 in small notes.

Keep the balance small. A foreign account most likely cannot withdraw it to a Brazilian card, so spend it down before leaving China.

**At a small vendor.** If the code is refused, it is probably a personal code. Try the other app, or ask for the merchant code: 商家收款码 (shāngjiā shōukuǎn mǎ).

**Fees.** Both apps add 3% on payments over ¥200 with a foreign card. Stay at ¥200 or under when you can, or split the payment.

**Not tested from Brazil.** A real merchant payment. The first one is at the airport in Shanghai; have the other app ready as the second try.

**The new shape:** Seoul Jan 11–16, Japan Jan 16 – Feb 1, Seoul again Feb 1–5, Hong Kong from Feb 5. Same day counts as before (Korea 9, Japan 16), so beds, food and the daily budget barely move. The cost is flights.

**Three new one-way tickets** (cheapest nonstop fares on Google Flights on 26 Nov 2026, shown in BRL, base fare for one adult):

1. Seoul → Tokyo, Sat Jan 16: from R$ 762 (ZIPAIR, 17:15 → 19:40 UTC+9, Narita). Jeju Air R$ 887 at 11:30, 14:50 or 16:20 UTC+9. The same Peach late flight to Haneda is R$ 874.
2. Osaka KIX → Seoul, Mon Feb 1: from R$ 782 (Air Busan, 12:00 → 14:10 UTC+9). Jeju Air R$ 794, Air Seoul R$ 796, Peach R$ 857.
3. Seoul → Hong Kong, Fri Feb 5: R$ 898 (HK Express, 12:25 UTC+9 → 15:30 UTC+8, in time for the eve fair). Leaving Thu Feb 4 at 21:50 UTC+9 is R$ 709.

Total: about **R$ 2.440** with the cheapest of each, about **R$ 2.580** with the easier times.

**Not confirmed: checked bags.** These are base fares and Google Flights could not filter these routes by checked bag. Jeju Air's own page lists the first 15 kg on a Basic fare at US$ 40–50 bought online. If all three legs need a paid bag, add up to roughly R$ 700–850 (at R$ 5,765 per US$, ECB rate of 26 Nov 2026). Check the bag line on the airline's checkout before paying.

**Tickets already paid that would go unused:** Peach MM808 Seoul → Tokyo on Jan 20 (R$ 999) and Greater Bay HB353 Osaka → Hong Kong on Feb 5 (R$ 1.113), R$ 2.112 together. Peach only allows changes on its Standard Plus fare, and the refund through the agency was already refused. Greater Bay's site lists date changes from HK$ 600–800 (≈ R$ 441–588) plus fare difference, but a route change from Osaka to Seoul is not offered there. Worth one message to the agency before giving it up.

**The variant that keeps the Hong Kong flight:** fly Seoul → Osaka on Thu Feb 4 afternoon (Air Seoul R$ 708 at 15:20 UTC+9, Jeju Air R$ 777, Peach R$ 840) and take HB353 on Feb 5 at 13:30 UTC+9 as booked. Cash is about R$ 190 less and the checked bag to Hong Kong is already paid, but it costs the last evening with the friend and adds a night near Kansai. Flying back on Feb 5 morning is too expensive (from R$ 1.234) and too tight.

**What else changes:**

- Jan 18 and 19 are work nights, now done from Tokyo (21:00–06:00 UTC+9, same clock as Seoul).
- The Hwacheon ice festival (9–31 Jan 2027) drops out: its weekend, Jan 16–17, becomes the move to Tokyo.
- Setsubun in Kyoto (Wed Feb 3) drops out. The Ado nights in Osaka (Jan 30–31) still fit, with the flight to Seoul the morning after.
- The K-ETA is multiple-entry and valid to 2029, so the second entry into Korea needs no new permit.

BRL uses 1 CNY = R$ 0,8263 (ECB reference rate, 27 Nov 2026).

**The game**

- GTA 6 came out on 19 Nov 2026 for PS5. It is not approved or sold in mainland China; locals buy it on a Hong Kong PSN account.
- There is no disc. The boxed edition holds a download code, so bringing a disc is not an option.
- The download is up to 212 GB: about 5 hours at 100 Mbps, about a day at 20 Mbps.

**Hotels**

- No listing found for SEYA, the YOLO hotels, or the central esports hotels names a PS5. The search could not read most Ctrip, Meituan, Dianping or Xiaohongshu pages, so a PS5 room may exist that was not found.
- **Guangzhi Cloud View Riverfront E-sports Hotel** (Jiangbei) lists one 90 m² suite on the 23rd floor with an RTX 5090 PC and a PS5, per Ctrip. Not confirmed: whether GTA 6 is installed, the price of that exact suite, and whether the console is a mainland or Hong Kong model.
- Logging in with your own account only works cleanly on a Hong Kong-model PS5. On a mainland model, adding a foreign account needs a workaround that wipes the console.

**PS5 lounges (主机游戏馆)**

- National reports from Nov 2026 put them at ¥50–60 (≈ R$ 41–50) an hour, with a 2-hour minimum for private rooms, and often full since launch. Book ahead.
- Chongqing candidates, all at Longhu Times Paradise Walk (龙湖时代天街) by Daping metro, Lines 1 and 2: 白金玩家·PS5·Switch, 耀咖主机游戏, 先知电玩·PS5. GTA 6 and prices there are not confirmed; check Dianping first.

**Message to send a hotel**

`您好，请问房间里有PS5吗？是国行还是港版？已经装好《GTA6》（侠盗猎车手6）了吗？可以登录我自己的海外PSN账号下载吗？房间网速大概多少？我持外国护照，可以入住吗？谢谢！`

It asks whether the room has a PS5, mainland or Hong Kong model, whether GTA 6 is installed, whether you can log in to your own PSN account, the internet speed, and whether a foreign passport can stay. For a lounge, swap 房间里 for 店里 and drop the passport sentence.

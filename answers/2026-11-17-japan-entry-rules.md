Japan and Brazil signed the extension on 24 Jul 2026. It runs from 30 Sep 2026 to 29 Sep 2029 and covers tourism and short visits. Paid work is not allowed. The waiver needs a passport with a chip (the ePassport symbol on the cover). Every Brazilian passport issued since Dec 2010 has one.

- **JESTA**, Japan's planned online travel permit, starts in 2028 at the earliest. Nothing to apply for.
- **Visit Japan Web** is optional and free. Fill it in before the flight and screenshot the QR codes for immigration and customs. Otherwise you fill in paper forms. Sites that charge are fakes.
- **Carry** the ticket for your flight out of Japan and the address of your first night's stay. Immigration may ask for them, and so may the airline at check-in.
- **Medicines:** up to 1 month's supply of ordinary prescription medicine is fine. More than that needs a free online import certificate before travel. Codeine and pseudoephedrine need a permit first. Amphetamine (Adderall) is banned. Check Brazilian cold medicines before you pack.
- **Departure tax** of ¥3,000 is already in the airline ticket.
- **Late landing at Haneda:** immigration works for night flights, but trains have stopped. Late buses leave Terminal 3 for Shinjuku and Ikebukuro at about 00:20, 01:00, 01:40 and 02:20 UTC+9 (as of Nov 2026), or take a taxi.
- **Lost passport:** an emergency passport issued abroad may have no chip, and then you would need a Japanese visa.

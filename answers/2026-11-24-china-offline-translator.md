**Tested in airplane mode, Wi-Fi off, on the Fold8 Ultra (Nov 2026). All six passed:**

1. Interpreter, voice, Portuguese → Chinese: text on screen and spoken aloud.
2. Interpreter, voice, Chinese → Portuguese.
3. Interpreter on the cover screen, so the other person reads the translation on the outside.
4. Interpreter, typing instead of speaking.
5. Google Translate, typed text, both directions.
6. Google Translate, camera on a Chinese menu.

**Talking to someone: Samsung Interpreter**

- Open it from the quick panel tile, or Settings › Galaxy AI › Interpreter.
- Set Portuguese ↔ Chinese and tap the microphone on the side that is speaking.
- For the cover screen: half-fold the phone, tap Conversation mode (the two chat bubbles), then the cover-screen icon at the top. Stand the phone on the table with the outside screen facing the other person.
- Noisy place: type instead of speaking.

**Menus, signs, typed text: Google Translate**

- Camera button, Chinese (Simplified) → Portuguese. If the result reads badly, switch the target to English.
- Google's two-way conversation mode needs a connection. Use Interpreter for voice.
- On hostel Wi-Fi without a VPN the app may wait on Google before it falls back to the offline pack. Turning Wi-Fi off for a moment forces offline.

**Packs on the phone**

- Both apps have the packs for every country on the trip (downloaded in Brazil, Nov 2026).
- If a pack goes missing: Interpreter packs are in Settings › Galaxy AI › Interpreter › Language packs for translation; Google's are under profile picture › Downloaded languages. Google's servers are blocked in China, so re-download over the roaming eSIM or a VPN, not hostel Wi-Fi.

**With the Galaxy Buds2 Pro (tried Nov 2026, works well)**

- Put the buds in and open Interpreter. You hear the other person's translation in your ears; your translation plays from the phone speaker for them.
- Conversation mode waits for the speaker to pause before it translates.
- For a guide, an announcement or a talk, Samsung documents a one-way Listening mode (the single chat bubble) that translates continuously into the buds. It is documented for the Buds3; not confirmed on the Buds2 Pro.
- Airplane mode switches Bluetooth off: turn Bluetooth back on.

**Not tested**

- WeChat's Scan › Translate as an online fallback that needs no VPN.
- Baidu Translate and Youdao were skipped: their offline packs appear to be Chinese ↔ English only.
